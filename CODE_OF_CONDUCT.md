# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link; the short version is below.

## Expected

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person. Accept that a reviewer who disagrees with you may simply disagree with you.

Technical disagreement is welcome and necessary here — the criteria decide what a badge means, and getting them right requires people arguing about them. Argue about the criteria.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report to [@2JIHAN](https://github.com/2JIHAN) through a private security advisory or a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or ban participants. Anyone who enforces this in bad faith faces the same consequences as anyone who breaks it.
